FDV Worship – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

FDV Worship is an app for worship teams: songs with chords, setlists, the Sunday rota, a team chat and messages to the sound/media team. This policy explains what the app stores and where.

Where your data lives

Everything your team creates is stored in Apple iCloud (CloudKit), in the iCloud account of the person who created the team, and shared only with the people who joined through that team's invite link. The developer does not run any server and cannot read your team's data.

What is stored

  • Your profile: first and last name, the roles you choose and, if you add one, a profile photo.
  • Team content: songs, chords, setlists, the Sunday rota, chat messages, voice messages and audio files, rehearsal recordings and messages to the media team.
  • Sign in with Apple: used only to identify you in iCloud; the app receives your name if you choose to share it.
  • On your device only: personal preferences such as transpositions, capo, language, notification settings and blocked people.

What is not collected

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no data sold or shared with third parties. The app does not use your location or contacts.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you record a voice message or a rehearsal; recordings are shared with your team.

Notifications

Notifications are delivered by iCloud when a teammate changes something. You can choose which kinds you receive in Settings → Notifications.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Delete Account. Your profile and your chat messages are deleted and you leave the team. If you created the team, the whole team and all its content are deleted for everyone. You can also stop using iCloud for the app from the iPhone's Settings.

Reporting content

Any chat message can be reported (long-press → Report) and its sender blocked. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours and offending content and users are removed.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
