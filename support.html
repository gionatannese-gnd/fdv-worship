Getting started

Sign in with Apple, then create a team or join one with the invite link (or QR code) a teammate shares from Settings.

Common questions

I don't see my team's changes. Check you're signed in to iCloud on the device and pull down on a list to refresh.

Too many notifications? Settings → Notifications → Choose What You Get.

Someone posts something inappropriate. Long-press the message → Report, and Block the person. The team's admin can delete any message.

Delete my account. Settings → Delete Account.
